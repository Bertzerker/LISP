Credit Token worth

Local credits only. Players pay for CPU time and LAN traffic. Administrator-owned servers are free. At the negative balance limit, servers stop and save their worlds.

Charge = CPU core-minutes ÷ CPU worth + (received + sent GiB) ÷ network worth. 100% CPU means one core. Average usage × elapsed time gives the charge; stopped servers are free. Changes apply to subsequent usage.
